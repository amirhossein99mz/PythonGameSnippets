Two noble families, equal in honor,
live in fair Verona, where our play begins.
An old hatred flares up into new violence,
and citizens’ hands are stained with citizens’ blood.
From these two enemies are born children
who fall in love, but fate is against them.
Their tragic deaths finally bring an end
to their parents’ bitter conflict.
The story of their doomed love,
and the persistence of their parents’ anger,
which nothing else could end,
will be told over the next two hours on our stage.
If you listen carefully,
whatever we leave out, our effort will try to supply.
